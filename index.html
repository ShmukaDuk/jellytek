01 — Who we are

A computer-vision company
that works on cards.

Small team, deep specialism.

We came out of automotive AI — cameras that have to work on every frame, in bad light, on a tiny power budget, without phoning home. We pointed that discipline at trading cards.

Our vision
Every card, on every device, recognised instantly and priced honestly — without sending a picture to somebody else's cloud.
