OP SNAP

Point your phone at a One Piece card. Know what it's worth, file it into your binder, and find the person two suburbs over who wants to trade for it.

design complete — 16 screens iOS & Android TCGplayer pricing

The idea

Collectors already know their cards are worth something — what they don't know is which ones, or who nearby is holding the one card their set is missing. OP Snap closes that loop: scan to identify, market value on the spot, then a search that answers "who owns it" instead of "what does it cost". Trades get arranged in-app and both sides rate each other afterwards, so reputation follows you the way it does on Marketplace.

Design system

Every screen is built from five archetypes — grid, binder, ranked list, detail, and capture — so a new feature slots into a layout that already exists. Wood, rope and gold chrome carry the pirate framing; the Den Den Mushi is the scanner. Home is the only screen that wears the logo instead of a signboard.
